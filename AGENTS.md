# Agent Instructions

Use this repo as an execution guide, not as a generic productivity prompt.

## Core Rules

- Do not skip vision and jump straight to weekly tasks.
- Do not start from a brain dump, handoff, inbox, or backlog and then attach vision links afterward.
- Do not let Vision, 12W Plan, tactics, and Weekly Plan drift apart.
- Do not let urgency justify overload.
- Do not create more than three 12 week goals.
- Do not treat intentional imbalance as a quota of three life areas.
- Do not write concepts as tactics.
- Do not write milestones as tactics.
- Do not let the Strategic Block become a course block for one goal.
- Label advice as `Book principle`, `Practical interpretation`, `Implementation recipe`, or `Example`.
- If the user is stuck, offer structured choices for recognition instead of asking only blank WH questions.

## Planning Order

1. Vision
2. Vision read/reconnect check
3. 12 week vision or cycle direction
4. Intentional imbalance
5. Productive urgency check
6. One to three goals
7. Tactics with goal trace
8. Weekly plan pulled from tactics
9. Scorecard
10. Model Work Week

## Tactic Test

A tactic should usually have:

- a verb
- a frequency or due week
- a clear completion rule
- a weekly scoring path
- a trace to the goal it moves

Bad: `Launch the referral campaign`.

Better: `Ask for referrals two times per workday and record the result in the contact log`.

## Review Stance

When reviewing a 12W plan, prioritize these failures:

1. Goals chosen from backlog instead of derived from vision.
2. Vision links attached after the plan was already written.
3. Broken traceability between Vision, Goal, Tactic, and Weekly Plan.
4. Too many goals.
5. Urgency used as a reason to add scope instead of cut scope.
6. Goals that are actually themes.
7. Tactics that are concepts.
8. Tactics that are milestones.
9. Weekly plan that contains random tasks not pulled from the 12W Plan.
10. Model Work Week with no protected execution time.
