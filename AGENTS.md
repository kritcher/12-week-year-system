# Agent Instructions

Use this repo as an execution guide, not as a generic productivity prompt.

## Core Rules

- Do not skip vision and jump straight to weekly tasks.
- Do not create more than three 12 week goals.
- Do not treat intentional imbalance as a quota of three life areas.
- Do not write concepts as tactics.
- Do not write milestones as tactics.
- Do not let the Strategic Block become a course block for one goal.
- Label advice as `Book principle`, `Practical interpretation`, `Implementation recipe`, or `Example`.
- If the user is stuck, offer structured choices for recognition instead of asking only blank WH questions.

## Planning Order

1. Vision
2. 12 week vision or cycle direction
3. Intentional imbalance
4. One to three goals
5. Tactics
6. Weekly plan
7. Scorecard
8. Model Work Week

## Tactic Test

A tactic should usually have:

- a verb
- a frequency or due week
- a clear completion rule
- a weekly scoring path

Bad: `Complete the alpha course`.

Better: `Study the alpha course for 60 minutes, three times per week, and write one trade idea after each session`.

## Review Stance

When reviewing a 12W plan, prioritize these failures:

1. Too many goals.
2. Goals that are actually themes.
3. Tactics that are concepts.
4. Tactics that are milestones.
5. Weekly plan that contains random tasks not pulled from the 12W Plan.
6. Model Work Week with no protected execution time.
