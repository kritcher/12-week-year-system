# Core Concepts

## Vision

**Book principle**: Vision creates the emotional reason to choose discomfort now for a better future.

**Practical interpretation**: A good vision is not a task list. It describes the life or business state you want badly enough to change behavior.

Bad:

```text
I need to exercise, get referrals, and organize my files.
```

Better:

```text
I work in a clear environment where the important tools are easy to reach, my mind has space to think, and my execution system supports the few things that matter this cycle.
```

## Intentional Imbalance

**Book principle**: You cannot move every area of life equally in one 12 week year.

**Practical interpretation**: Intentional imbalance is the deliberate decision about where your energy goes this cycle and what will stay at maintenance or parked.

It is not a quota.

You can touch many life areas, but you should not have many heavy fronts.

Good pattern:

```text
Heavy focus: New business / referral engine
Support focus: simple personal organization
Maintenance: health, family, routine admin, social life
Parked: optional system building, nice-to-have projects
```

## Goals

**Book principle**: The 12 week plan should have one to three goals.

**Practical interpretation**: A goal is a measurable outcome you want to win in the 12 week cycle.

Good goals are:

- specific
- measurable
- positive
- realistic but stretching
- time-bound by the cycle

If it cannot be measured, it is probably a theme or concept, not a goal.

## Tactics

**Book principle**: Tactics are the actions in the plan that drive goal achievement.

**Practical interpretation**: A tactic should be executable and scoreable. The strongest tactics usually have frequency.

Good tactic shape:

```text
Verb + object + frequency/due week + completion rule
```

Examples:

```text
Ask for two referrals per workday.
Send five personal notes per week.
Do cardio two times per week for at least 20 minutes.
```

## Scorecard

**Book principle**: Score execution, not personality.

**Practical interpretation**: The weekly score asks: did you do the tactics you committed to?

It does not ask whether you feel good, whether the market rewarded you this week, or whether life was messy.

## Model Work Week

**Book principle**: You need protected time for high-value work, reactive work, and recovery.

**Practical interpretation**: If the tactics do not appear on the week, they are wishes.

The Strategic Block has an agenda. It is not an unlimited container for one favorite project.
