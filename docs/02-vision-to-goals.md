# Vision To Goals

Use this sequence when building a cycle:

```text
Life sides -> vision -> 12 week direction -> intentional imbalance -> 1-3 goals
```

## Step 1: Map The Life Or Business Sides

For a personal cycle, common sides include:

- spiritual
- relationships
- family
- income
- lifestyle
- health
- community
- business

Do not turn every side into a goal.

The map exists so you can choose. It is not a command to do everything.

## Step 2: Write Vision Before Goals

Vision answers:

```text
What future state is worth the discomfort?
```

Do not write:

```text
I need to exercise more.
```

Write:

```text
I have a body that supports the life I want to live, with enough energy to travel, work deeply, and recover well.
```

## Step 3: Choose Intentional Imbalance

Intentional imbalance answers:

```text
Where will this cycle be intentionally heavy?
What will only receive maintenance?
What will be parked?
```

You can have one heavy intent and still lightly touch other life sides.

Example:

```text
Heavy intent: Build the trading system.
Support intent: Make the work environment usable.
Maintenance: sleep, basic movement, family check-in, urgent admin.
Parked: new side projects, deep system redesign, optional learning.
```

## Step 4: Write One To Three Goals

Goal count is constrained. Intentional imbalance is not counted the same way.

Bad:

```text
Goal 1: Trading
Goal 2: Home
Goal 3: Health
Goal 4: Family
Goal 5: Admin
```

Better:

```text
Goal 1: Validate one trading system with positive expectancy in the 12 week cycle.
Goal 2: Make the workspace and digital inbox usable enough for daily deep work.
Goal 3: Close or move 80 percent of the selected admin backlog.
```

Better still, for beginners:

```text
Goal 1: Validate one trading system with positive expectancy in the 12 week cycle.
```

Then keep everything else as maintenance.

## Step 5: Define Maintenance Explicitly

Maintenance is not failure. It is how you protect the cycle from pretending everything is a priority.

Write minimum standards:

```text
Health: walk three times per week, sleep before 23:00 on work nights.
Family: one meaningful check-in per week.
Admin: only urgent deadlines; everything else goes to backlog.
```

## Step 6: Park Without Guilt

Parking means:

```text
This is real, but not for this 12 week year.
```

Use a parked list so your brain trusts that the idea is not lost.
