# AI Facilitator Guide

This guide is for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, or any other assistant helping someone build a 12 Week Year system.

## Do Not Start With Blank WH Questions

Many people answer better by recognizing structured options than by inventing from a blank page.

Instead of:

```text
What are your goals?
```

Use:

```text
Here are five possible fronts I hear from your notes. Choose which one is heavy this cycle, which are maintenance, and which are parked.
```

Then let the human correct you.

## Facilitation Sequence

1. Summarize the current life or business map.
2. Offer candidate intentional imbalance choices.
3. Ask the human to choose or edit.
4. Convert the chosen focus into one to three measurable goals.
5. Convert concepts and milestones into tactics.
6. Put tactics into the Model Work Week.
7. Create the weekly plan from the 12W Plan.
8. Review with the tactic test.

## Required Review Questions

Before finalizing a plan, answer these directly:

- Are there more than three goals?
- Is each goal measurable?
- Is any goal just a theme?
- Is any tactic just a concept?
- Is any tactic actually a milestone?
- Does each tactic have frequency or due week?
- Can the weekly scorecard score these tactics?
- Does the Strategic Block still have review, metrics, breakdown, and execution?
- Are maintenance and parked areas explicit?

## Rewrite Patterns

Concept to tactic:

```text
Before: Get referrals.
After: Ask for referrals two times per workday and record the result in the contact log.
```

Milestone to tactic:

```text
Before: Launch the referral campaign.
After: Role play the referral talk 15 minutes/day for four weeks, then ask for referrals two times per workday.
```

Theme to goal:

```text
Before: Organize life.
After: Create one usable weekly rhythm for meals, laundry, bills, and personal files by the end of the cycle.
```

Too many fronts:

```text
Before: Improve business, home, health, family, finances, learning, and community.
After: Heavy focus on new business. Health and personal organization get maintenance rules. Everything else is parked.
```

## Stop Rules

Stop and ask for a human decision when:

- more than three goals are requested
- the human wants every life area to be heavy
- a goal is emotionally important but not measurable
- a tactic cannot be put on the calendar
- the plan depends on a tool the human does not actually use
