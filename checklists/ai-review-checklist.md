# AI Review Checklist

Use this checklist before accepting an AI-generated 12 Week Year plan.

## Red Flags

- [ ] AI asked for goals before discussing vision.
- [ ] AI started from a brain dump, handoff, inbox, or backlog.
- [ ] AI attached vision links after goals and tactics were already written.
- [ ] AI created more than three goals.
- [ ] AI treated intentional imbalance as exactly three life areas.
- [ ] AI listed every life area as active.
- [ ] AI wrote vague tactics like `study`, `exercise`, `organize`, or `research`.
- [ ] AI wrote milestones as tactics.
- [ ] AI put a course inside the whole Strategic Block.
- [ ] AI created a weekly to-do list not pulled from the 12W Plan.
- [ ] AI added Weekly Plan items without source tactic IDs.
- [ ] AI did not specify maintenance and parked areas.

## Required Fixes

For each bad tactic, ask:

```text
Is this a concept, milestone, or real action?
What is the frequency?
How will I score it this week?
Where does it live on the calendar?
```

For each extra goal, ask:

```text
Is this truly a 12 week goal, or should it be maintenance or parked?
```

For each goal, ask:

```text
Which vision sentence or theme did this goal come from?
Did this goal come from vision, or from backlog pressure?
What future state does this move in the next 12 weeks?
```

For the Strategic Block, ask:

```text
Does this block still review vision, metrics, breakdown, and highest-leverage tactics?
```

For each weekly item, ask:

```text
Which 12W tactic did this come from?
Which 12W goal does that tactic move?
Which vision theme created that goal?
```
